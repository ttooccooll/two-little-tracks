About

Two Little Tracks is an electronic music project exploring the boundaries between ambient textures and rhythmic experimentation. Drawing influence from Berlin techno, Japanese ambient, and vintage analog synthesis.

Each track is crafted with meticulous attention to sound design, creating immersive sonic landscapes that blur the line between dancing and dreaming.
